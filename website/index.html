Nevumi

An open source music player for Android and Linux. Play local files and your Navidrome or Subsonic server, and choose what happens next.

A player with the controls you want

One library

Play local files or connect Navidrome and Subsonic. Match local and server copies, search everything, and download music for offline playback.

Queues that stay with you

Keep requests in Up next. Save live queues with their history and position, switch between them, or restore a recovery snapshot.

Your sound

Gapless playback, crossfade, independent speed and pitch, an equalizer, AutoEQ headphone profiles and a sleep timer.

MilkDrop, built in

Over 10,000 presets, available offline through projectM. Search the collection, lock a preset or play only your favorites. It can also run behind Now playing.

Like the visuals in this header? Find all five in the app's Nevumi preset collection, where they react to your music.

Paired devices

Pair by QR code or a manual code. Sync playlists, favorites, ratings, listens and saved queue contents. Each device keeps its own playback position.

Keep your library in order

Edit tags and artwork, find MusicBrainz metadata, create smart playlists, and scrobble to your server, Last.fm or ListenBrainz.

Linux x86_64

Download for Linux
Install on Arch Linux

Download the signing key and check its fingerprint:

curl -fsSL -o nevumi.asc https://nevumi.app/nevumi.asc
gpg --show-keys --fingerprint nevumi.asc

5948F17F1611CC2B995BC6121F0D542126D514AB

Import and trust the key:

sudo pacman-key --add nevumi.asc
sudo pacman-key --lsign-key 5948F17F1611CC2B995BC6121F0D542126D514AB

Add the repository, then install:

sudo pacman-conf --repo-list | grep -qxF 'nevumi' ||
  printf '%s\n' '' '[nevumi]' 'SigLevel = Required DatabaseRequired' \
    'Server = https://nevumi.app/arch/$arch' |
  sudo tee -a /etc/pacman.conf >/dev/null

sudo pacman -Syu nevumi

Why another music player?

I couldn't find one with all the features I wanted. Namida came closest, but I didn't like its license. So I started Nevumi: an independent app for local music and music servers, with a Material You interface.
